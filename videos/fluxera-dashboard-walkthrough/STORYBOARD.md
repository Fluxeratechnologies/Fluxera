## Video direction

Use the actual dashboard as the proof. Frame it within a dark ink field with cream typography and burnt-orange highlights. Move between screenshots with brief crossfades, small camera pushes into the evidence region, and a visible cursor only for the approval beat. Keep all on-screen data labeled as illustrative synthetic data. Burn the narration captions into the lower safe area. The closing TV power-off effect resolves to a clean Fluxera logo and getfluxera.com.
