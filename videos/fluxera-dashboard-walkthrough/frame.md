# Frame design

- Canvas: 1920 × 1080, 16:9.
- Palette: ink `#111111`, cream `#F3EEE3`, burnt orange `#D9541E`, white for dashboard screenshots.
- Type: clean sans serif, large concise title, readable captions in the lower safe area.
- Product evidence: actual Fluxera React dashboard captures from an isolated synthetic API; frame as a product surface, never as anonymous stock UI.
- Motion: brief crossfades, restrained camera pushes, one deliberate pointer/approval beat, and a final CRT-style power-off line.
- Trust cue: small persistent `Illustrative synthetic data` label wherever dashboard evidence is visible.
- End: clean Fluxera mark, wordmark, and `getfluxera.com` on ink.
